**Starfall Harbor — Duel of the Nine Lanterns.** A lantern-card duel: you are Rin, Keeper of the Harbor Light, facing Vey, Captain of the Drowned Fleet. 36 lantern cards in 3 tides (Amber ☀ beats Teal 🌊 beats Violet ☾ beats Amber), 5-card hands, 20 light each, oil pays for cards (1–3) and refills every round. Every hand is a decision: the Omen telegraphs Vey's favored tide (honest 3 times in 4), and combos chain — Twin Lights +2, Full Spectrum +4 and heal 2, High Tide +1 oil. First to sink the rival's 20 light takes the harbor. A tied tide grants both sides +1 oil. Keyboard 1–5 + mouse, synthesized WebAudio sounds, title / table / win / lose screens, confetti on victory.
