★ STARFALL HARBOR ★

Duel of the Nine Lanterns

You are Rin, Keeper of the Harbor Light. Across the table sits Vey, Captain of the Drowned Fleet. Each round you both play one lantern card. Higher power wins the tide and sinks the rival's light. First to sink all 20 light takes the harbor.

☀ Amber > 🌊 Teal > ☾ Violet > ☀ Amber — a winning tide is +3 power.
Oil pays for cards (1–3). It refills +1 every round. Broke? The harbor lends your cheapest card free.
Combos: Twin Lights +2 · Full Spectrum +4 & heal 2 · High Tide +1 oil.
Read the Omen: Vey telegraphs the tide he favors — 3 times in 4 it is true. Counter it.
